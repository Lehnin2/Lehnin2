### 04 · built

**[h12](https://github.com/Lehnin2/h12)** — *Guardian of the Gulf*
Where to fish around the Gulf of Gabès, and where not to. Zone scoring over bathymetry, pollution, marine weather, satellite data and fleet pressure, with eco-routing, community reports, and an SOS nobody hopes to use.
<sub>`FastAPI` `React` `TypeScript` `Leaflet` `SQLite` `PWA`</sub>

**[restaurant-monituring](https://github.com/Lehnin2/restaurant-monituring)** — *ServiceEye*
A dining room made legible. Zone-based detection turns cameras into occupancy, dwell time and table state; the dashboard changes while you're looking at it.
<sub>`YOLOv8` `OpenCV` `FastAPI` `React` `PostgreSQL` `WebSockets` `Docker`</sub>

**[Automated-Comliance-Check](https://github.com/Lehnin2/Automated-Comliance-Check)**
Financial slide decks, read by something that doesn't get bored. Agents take the deck apart, hold it against the rules, and leave behind a trace of how each decision was reached.
<sub>`LangGraph` `Gemini` `Groq` `FastAPI` `React`</sub>

**[faceLiveness](https://github.com/Lehnin2/faceLiveness)**
Deciding whether the face in front of the camera is attached to someone currently in the room. Gaze challenges, blink detection, anti-spoofing.
<sub>`PyTorch` `ResNet18` `MediaPipe` `OpenCV`</sub>

**[blueMind](https://github.com/Lehnin2/blueMind)**
Fish recognition, pollution monitoring, marine flora, navigation, legal help. Six ideas sharing one repository. They didn't all arrive at the same time, and they didn't all arrive finished.
