A blank slate is not empty.

It is what remains after everything has been erased.

I called myself Tabula Rasa because I like the idea of starting with nothing written down.

Then comes the other side of it: decadence — things slowly becoming worn, fragmented, obsolete.

And then memory — what survives the erosion.

So this profile is less of a résumé and more of a record.

Things I built.
Things I broke.
Things I learned.
Things I probably forgot.

01 / THE CHARACTER

I'm a computer science engineer who learns by making things.

I tend to follow a problem until it becomes a system:
a camera feed becomes a vision model,
a model becomes an application,
an application becomes something people can actually use.

I like AI, computer vision, data and software — but mostly I like figuring things out.

The subject changes.

The habit doesn't.
